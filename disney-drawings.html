✏️ Simple Disney Drawings

A step-by-step drawing book for 7-year-old artists

👋 How to use this book

Each character is built from simple shapes — circles, ovals, and triangles — that stack together to make the final drawing. Look at the little step pictures: red lines show the NEW shape to draw in that step, and gray lines show shapes from earlier steps. Draw lightly with pencil first so you can erase, and don't worry if it doesn't look perfect — that's what makes your art YOURS!

🛒 What You'll Need

  • Pencil (with a good eraser)
  • Plain white paper
  • A black marker or fine-tip pen (for tracing the final lines)
  • Crayons, colored pencils, or markers (for coloring)
How to read the steps: red = new line to draw · gray = lines from earlier steps · the final picture shows everything in black.

1Mickey Mouse

SHAPES: 3 circles + a peanut snout
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a big circle in the middle of your page — that's Mickey's head.
Step 2. Add two round circle ears on top, one on each side.
Step 3. In the lower half of the face, draw a tall peanut-shaped snout.
Step 4. Draw two tall oval eyes at the top of the snout.
Step 5. Add a fat oval nose right in the middle, color it black.
Step 6. Draw a big curved smile across the bottom of the snout.

💡 Tip: Mickey's ears are always FULL circles, even when he turns his head!

2Minnie Mouse

SHAPES: Mickey's face + a polka-dot bow
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a big circle for Minnie's head.
Step 2. Add two round circle ears on top.
Step 3. Add the peanut snout in the lower face.
Step 4. Draw two oval eyes (with curly eyelashes!) and an oval nose.
Step 5. Add a curved smile across the snout.
Step 6. On top of her head, draw a bow — two triangles meeting at a small circle knot. Add polka dots!

💡 Tip: Minnie's bow is usually red with white polka dots, but pick any color you like!

3Olaf

SHAPES: 3 stacked snowballs + carrot nose + sticks
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a small circle near the top — Olaf's head.
Step 2. Below that, a slightly bigger circle — his belly.
Step 3. Below that, the BIGGEST circle — his bottom snowball.
Step 4. On the head, draw a long pointy carrot nose with little stripes.
Step 5. Add two dot eyes (with eyebrows!), a toothy smile, and 3 hair twigs.
Step 6. Add stick arms with twiggy fingers, and 3 black coal buttons on his belly.

💡 Tip: Olaf is all white! Outline him in black and color the carrot orange.

4Winnie the Pooh

SHAPES: Round head + tubby tummy
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a round circle for the head.
Step 2. Add two small bump ears on top.
Step 3. Below the head, draw a big round tummy oval.
Step 4. Add two short arms on each side and two stubby legs at the bottom.
Step 5. For the face: a small oval nose, two dot eyes, a smile, and a curve across the top of his belly for his red shirt.

💡 Tip: Pooh's shirt is short — his round tummy peeks out underneath!

5Piglet

SHAPES: BIG head + tiny body + pig snout
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a big round head — bigger than the body!
Step 2. On top, add two tall pointy triangle ears (bent at the tips).
Step 3. In the middle of the face, draw a small oval pig snout with two dot nostrils.
Step 4. Add two dot eyes above the snout.
Step 5. Below, draw a small rounded body with short arms and legs and a curly tail.

💡 Tip: Piglet is mostly pink with a magenta-striped shirt.

6Stitch

SHAPES: Big head + bunny ears + oval body
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a round head, slightly wider at the top.
Step 2. On top, draw two big bunny-style ears (long ovals tilted outward) with smaller ovals inside.
Step 3. Below the head, draw a fat oval body.
Step 4. Add two short arms and two short legs.
Step 5. For the face: two BIG round eyes, a small triangle nose, a wide smile, and a curve across his tummy.

💡 Tip: Stitch is bright blue with a lighter blue tummy and pink inside his ears.

7Nemo

SHAPES: Oval fish + triangle tail + stripes
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a wide oval shape for his body.
Step 2. On the back end, add a tail with two pointy triangles.
Step 3. Add a small bumpy fin on top and two small fins on the bottom.
Step 4. Near the front, draw one big round eye and a small smile.
Step 5. Add 3 white stripes outlined in black across his body.

💡 Tip: Nemo is orange and white. His left fin is smaller — his 'lucky fin'!

8Dory

SHAPES: Oval blue fish + forked tail
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a wide oval body, slightly bigger than Nemo's.
Step 2. On the back, add a forked tail (two pointy fins like a fork).
Step 3. Add a top fin curving along her back and a small bottom fin.
Step 4. Near the front, draw a big round eye and a small smile.
Step 5. Draw a curved stripe along her back — that will be the black stripe!

💡 Tip: Dory is bright blue with a black stripe and yellow fin tips.

9BB-8

SHAPES: Big circle body + dome head
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a big circle for his body ball.
Step 2. On top, draw a dome (half-circle) for his head.
Step 3. Add a small antenna sticking up from the dome.
Step 4. On the dome, draw a big circle eye-camera and a smaller circle next to it.
Step 5. On the body, draw a few medium circles with small dots inside for details.

💡 Tip: BB-8 is white with orange and gray details. The body ROLLS — head stays on top!

10Baymax

SHAPES: Tiny head + marshmallow body
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a small round head at the top.
Step 2. Below the head, draw a HUGE rounded marshmallow body.
Step 3. Add two short fat arms with mitten hands.
Step 4. Add two short fat legs at the bottom.
Step 5. For the face: just two black dots connected by a thin line. That's it — Baymax keeps it simple!

💡 Tip: Baymax is all white. His face is just two dots and a line!

11Pascal

SHAPES: Curved body + round head + spiral tail
What it looks like
All shapes together
Step 1. Draw a curved banana-shape body across the page.
Step 2. At one end, add a round head.
Step 3. On the other end, draw a curly spiral tail.
Step 4. Add four little feet, each with 3 small toes.
Step 5. On the head, draw ONE big round eye, a curved smile, and bumpy crests along his back.

💡 Tip: Pascal is bright green and changes color with his mood — try different colors!

🌟 You did it!

Once you've drawn a character with pencil, trace over the lines with your black marker. Wait for the marker to dry, then erase the pencil lines underneath. Now color it in! Try drawing the same character a few times — yours will get better and better. Keep practicing, keep creating!
