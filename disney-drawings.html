✏️ 20 Simple Disney Drawings

A step-by-step drawing book for 7-year-old artists

👋 How to use this book

Each character starts with simple shapes — circles, ovals, and triangles — that build into the final drawing. Follow the steps in order, draw lightly with pencil first so you can erase, and don't worry if it doesn't look perfect. Every artist's drawings look a little different, and that's what makes them special!

🛒 What You'll Need (for every drawing)

  • Pencil (with a good eraser)
  • Plain white paper
  • A black marker or fine-tip pen (for tracing the final lines)
  • Crayons, colored pencils, or markers (for coloring)

1Mickey Mouse

SHAPES: 3 circles + a peanut shape
  1. Draw a big circle in the middle of your page — that's Mickey's head.
  2. On top of the head, add two round circle ears (one on the left, one on the right).
  3. In the bottom half of the face, draw a tall peanut shape — that's his snout.
  4. Inside the top of the snout, draw two tall ovals close together for his eyes. Color in tiny pupil dots.
  5. At the very top of the snout, draw a fat oval and color it black — that's his nose.
  6. Add a big curved smile from one side of the snout to the other. Add a small tongue inside.

💡 Tip: Mickey's ears are always FULL circles, even when he turns his head!

2Minnie Mouse

SHAPES: Mickey + a polka-dot bow
  1. Draw Minnie the same way you drew Mickey: big circle for the head, two circle ears on top.
  2. Add the peanut snout, two oval eyes (this time give her long curly eyelashes!), the black nose, and a smile.
  3. Now for her bow: draw two triangles meeting in the middle, on top of her head, pointing in opposite directions.
  4. Add a small circle in the middle where the triangles meet (the bow's knot).
  5. Decorate the bow with little polka dots.

💡 Tip: Minnie's bow is usually red with white polka dots, but you can color it any color you like!

3Donald Duck

SHAPES: Round head + sailor hat + big beak
  1. Draw a round head, a little wider at the top than the bottom.
  2. On top of the head, draw a half-circle sailor hat with a flat band across the bottom of it.
  3. Add a long ribbon hanging down the back of the hat.
  4. Draw a big oval beak sticking out from the front of the face.
  5. Add a curved line across the middle of the beak to split the top and bottom.
  6. Above the beak, draw two tall oval eyes. Add small pupils inside.
  7. (Optional) Draw a round body below the head with a sailor collar — two pointy triangles.

💡 Tip: Donald's hat and shirt are blue, his beak is yellow.

4Daisy Duck

SHAPES: Donald's head + flower bow + eyelashes
  1. Draw a round head and a long oval beak just like Donald.
  2. On top of her head, draw a big flower-shape bow (5 rounded petals around a small circle in the middle).
  3. Add two oval eyes with long curly eyelashes.
  4. Draw a curved line across the beak to split top and bottom.
  5. Add three little curls of feathery hair sticking up from the top of her head.

💡 Tip: Color her bow pink or purple — Daisy loves bright colors!

5Goofy

SHAPES: Long oval head + droopy ears + tall hat
  1. Draw a long oval shape, tilted slightly — that's Goofy's head.
  2. Add a long pointy snout sticking out from the front, like a dog's nose.
  3. Put a big black oval at the tip for his nose.
  4. Draw two long floppy ears hanging down from each side of his head.
  5. On top of his head, draw a tall, lumpy hat (it leans a little — like a sock).
  6. Add two small round eyes near the top of the head, with the eyebrows raised up.
  7. Draw two big buck teeth poking out under the snout, with a smile around them.

💡 Tip: Goofy's hat is green and his ears are floppy — make them really hang down!

6Pluto

SHAPES: Oval body + round head + droopy ears
  1. Draw a long oval lying on its side — that's Pluto's body.
  2. At the front, add a round head with a long pointy snout (like a teardrop).
  3. Put a big black nose at the tip of the snout.
  4. Draw two long droopy ears hanging from the top of his head.
  5. Add four short legs underneath the body.
  6. Draw a long curved tail sticking up from the back.
  7. Add a big collar (a band around his neck with a round tag).
  8. Two small dot eyes and a smiling mouth.

💡 Tip: Pluto is yellow-orange with a green collar. His tongue often hangs out!

7Winnie the Pooh

SHAPES: Round head + tubby tummy
  1. Draw a round circle head.
  2. On top, add two small bumps (rounded ears).
  3. Below the head, draw a big round tummy shape (he's a cuddly bear!).
  4. Add two short arms on each side and two stubby legs at the bottom.
  5. For the face: a small black oval nose right in the middle, two dot eyes above it, and a smile under it.
  6. Draw his red shirt — a curve across the top of his belly.

💡 Tip: Pooh's shirt is short — his round tummy shows underneath!

8Piglet

SHAPES: Big head + tiny body + pig snout
  1. Draw a big round head — bigger than the body!
  2. In the middle of the face, draw a small oval pig snout with two dot nostrils.
  3. On top of his head, draw two tall pointy triangle ears, bent forward at the tip.
  4. Add two dot eyes above the snout.
  5. Draw a tiny rounded body below the head.
  6. Add two short arms and two small legs.
  7. Draw a curly little pig tail behind him.
  8. Stripes on his shirt — a few wavy lines across the body.

💡 Tip: Piglet is mostly pink with a magenta-striped shirt.

9Eeyore

SHAPES: Long oval body + droopy donkey ears
  1. Draw a long oval lying sideways — that's his body.
  2. At one end, add a smaller oval head sticking up.
  3. Draw two long droopy donkey ears hanging down on each side of the head.
  4. Add four short legs under the body.
  5. For the face: a small oval nose, two dot eyes, and a curved sad-looking mouth.
  6. On top of his head, draw a little tuft of hair (a few wavy lines).
  7. Don't forget his tail — a thin line with a triangle/bow shape on the end.

💡 Tip: Eeyore is gray-blue and always looks a little sad. His pink ribbon tail is his special accessory.

10Tigger

SHAPES: Oval body + round head + curly tail + stripes!
  1. Draw a round head.
  2. Add two small triangle cat ears on top.
  3. Below the head, draw an oval body — a little wider in the middle.
  4. Add four legs and arms — Tigger is bouncy!
  5. Draw a long curly springy tail behind him (like a question mark).
  6. Face: oval nose, two big eyes, big toothy smile (you can show one or two teeth).
  7. Now add stripes — wavy lines all over his body, head, and tail.
  8. Add a few short whiskers on his face.

💡 Tip: Tigger is orange with black stripes — and a white belly. His stripes go in different directions!

11Olaf

SHAPES: 3 stacked snowballs + carrot nose + sticks
  1. Draw a small round head circle near the top of your page.
  2. Below that, draw a slightly bigger circle (his belly).
  3. Below THAT, draw the biggest circle (his bottom).
  4. On the head, draw a long carrot nose pointing forward — a triangle with little stripes.
  5. Add two black dot eyes above the carrot, with little eyebrows.
  6. Draw a big toothy grin under the carrot — his teeth show!
  7. Coming out of his belly, draw two stick arms with twiggy fingers.
  8. On top of his head, add 3 little twigs sticking up like hair.
  9. Add 3 black coal buttons down his belly.

💡 Tip: Olaf is all white! Just outline him in black and color the carrot nose orange.

12Stitch

SHAPES: Big head + bunny-style ears + oval body
  1. Draw a round head, slightly wider at the top.
  2. On top, draw two big rabbit-like ears (long ovals tilted outward).
  3. Inside each ear, draw a smaller oval (the inner ear).
  4. Below the head, draw a rounded body — a fat oval.
  5. Add two arms with little claws and two short legs at the bottom.
  6. Face: two BIG round eyes, a small triangle nose, and a wide smile (his teeth show).
  7. Draw a curve across his tummy to show his lighter belly.

💡 Tip: Stitch is bright blue with a lighter blue tummy and pink inside his ears.

13Nemo

SHAPES: Oval fish + triangle tail + stripes
  1. Draw a wide oval shape — that's his body.
  2. On the back end, add a triangle (or two pointy triangles) for the tail.
  3. On top, draw a small bumpy fin.
  4. On the bottom, draw a small fin near the front and another near the tail.
  5. Near the front, draw one big round eye with a smaller circle inside (pupil).
  6. Add a small smile right below the eye.
  7. Now the stripes! Draw 3 white stripes outlined in black across his body — one near the head, one in the middle, one near the tail.

💡 Tip: Nemo is orange and white. His left fin is smaller than his right (his "lucky fin")!

14Dory

SHAPES: Oval blue fish + yellow tail
  1. Draw a wide oval body, slightly bigger than Nemo.
  2. On the back, add a tail with two pointy fins (like a fork).
  3. Add a top fin (a curved triangle along the back) and a small bottom fin.
  4. Near the front, draw one big round eye.
  5. Add a small smile right under it.
  6. Draw a curved line along her back — this will be the black stripe.

💡 Tip: Dory's body is bright blue, the stripe along her back is black, and her tail and fin tips are yellow.

15Bambi

SHAPES: Oval body + round head + pointy ears + thin legs
  1. Draw an oval body in the middle of the page.
  2. At one end, add a smaller round head sticking up.
  3. On top of the head, add two leaf-shaped pointy ears.
  4. Draw four long thin legs underneath — Bambi is wobbly!
  5. Add a tiny tail at the back.
  6. Face: a black oval nose, two big dot eyes (with eyelashes!), and a small smile.
  7. Sprinkle small white spots all over his back.

💡 Tip: Bambi is light brown with white spots and a white belly. Make his legs really thin like sticks!

16Dumbo

SHAPES: Round head + GIANT floppy ears + curvy trunk
  1. Draw a round head in the middle of the page.
  2. On each side of the head, draw a HUGE floppy ear (much bigger than his head!).
  3. Below the head, draw a small chubby body.
  4. From the front of his face, draw a long curvy trunk reaching down or up.
  5. Add four short stumpy legs.
  6. Draw a tiny tail at the back.
  7. Face: two big eyes with long eyelashes, and a small smile under the trunk.
  8. (Optional) Add a tiny yellow hat on his head.

💡 Tip: Dumbo is gray. The inside of his ears is pink. The bigger the ears, the cuter he looks!

17WALL-E

SHAPES: Cube body + binocular eyes + treads
  1. Draw a square (or short rectangle) — that's his body box.
  2. At the bottom, draw two flat ovals on each side — those are his treads (like little tank tracks).
  3. Add lines across the treads to show the rolling parts.
  4. On top of the body, draw a thin horizontal rectangle — that's his neck.
  5. On top of the neck, draw two circles next to each other (like binoculars) — those are his eyes!
  6. Add a smaller circle inside each eye for the pupils.
  7. On each side of his body, draw a thin arm with a 3-fingered claw hand.
  8. Decorate the box with little squares, circles, and dirt smudges.

💡 Tip: WALL-E is yellow and dirty-brown. Don't worry about it being too neat — he's a hardworking robot!

18BB-8

SHAPES: Big circle body + half-circle head
  1. Draw a big circle in the middle of the page — that's his body ball.
  2. On top, draw a half-circle (a dome) — that's his head.
  3. Add a small antenna sticking up from the top of his head.
  4. On the head, draw a big circle for his main eye-camera and a smaller circle next to it.
  5. Decorate his body ball: draw 3 medium circles around the body.
  6. Add small dots and details inside each big body circle.
  7. Don't forget the line where his head sits on the body.

💡 Tip: BB-8 is white with orange and gray details. The body ROLLS while the head stays on top!

19Baymax

SHAPES: Marshmallow body + tiny head
  1. Draw a small round head at the top.
  2. Below the head, draw a HUGE rounded body — like a giant marshmallow or pillow.
  3. On each side of the body, draw two short fat arms ending in mitten hands.
  4. At the bottom, draw two short fat legs.
  5. For his face: just two black dots for eyes, connected by a thin black line across his head. That's it — Baymax has the simplest face!
  6. (Optional) Add a small red heart on his chest.

💡 Tip: Baymax is all white. His face is just two dots and a line — keep it super simple!

20Pascal

SHAPES: Curly chameleon body + big eye + curly tail
  1. Draw an oval body shape, slightly curved like a banana.
  2. At one end, add a round head.
  3. On the other end, draw a curly spiral tail.
  4. Draw four little feet sticking out — each foot has 3 small toes.
  5. On the head, draw ONE big round eye (chameleons have huge eyes!).
  6. Add a small mouth — just a curved smile.
  7. Draw a little bumpy crest along his back (small triangle bumps).
  8. (Optional) Add small dots all over his back for chameleon scales.

💡 Tip: Pascal is bright green and changes colors depending on his mood — try drawing him in different colors!

🌟 You did it!

Once you've drawn a character with pencil, trace over the lines with your black marker. Wait for the marker to dry, then erase the pencil lines underneath. Now color it in! Try drawing the same character a few times — yours will get better and better. Keep practicing, keep creating!
