1. Pandy & Cakey Birthday Goodie Bags

⏱ Time: 30–40 min 👶 Age: 3+ with help ⭐ Difficulty: Easy

🛒 Supplies

  • 2 white paper gift bags (one for Pandy, one for Cakey)
  • White craft foam
  • Black marker
  • 2 heart gems (for noses)
  • Googly eyes (4)
  • Black pipe cleaners (whiskers)
  • Tissue paper (assorted)
  • Glue stick
  • Magenta, pink, purple, and turquoise foil sheets (Cakey's "cupcake wrapper")
  • Sprinkles or small treats to fill the bags

Pandy Goodie Bag — Steps

  1. Cut two triangles from white foam — these are Pandy's ears.
  2. Color the tip of each ear black with the marker (Pandy's signature look).
  3. Glue the ears to the top of the bag.
  4. Draw Pandy's mouth in the center of the bag.
  5. Glue a heart gem above the mouth as the nose.
  6. Cut a black pipe cleaner into 4 short pieces and glue them on as whiskers (2 per side).
  7. Glue on two googly eyes.
  8. Fill the bag with goodies (stickers, snacks, small toys), then tuff tissue paper on top.

Cakey Goodie Bag — Steps

  1. Cut the top of the second bag into Cakey's rounded cupcake-frosting shape.
  2. Glue on a heart-gem nose, two googly eyes, and a drawn mouth.
  3. Cut pipe cleaner pieces and glue 3 to the top of the head and 2 on each side as whiskers (these are Cakey's "sprinkles").
  4. Cut the magenta foil into a rounded shape with little triangles at the bottom — this is the cupcake wrapper. Glue under the face.
  5. Repeat with pink, purple, and turquoise foil layered up the bag for a striped wrapper effect.
  6. Fill with sprinkles and treats, top with bright tissue paper.
👩‍🦰 Grown-Up Helper: Cutting the foam triangles, the bag's frosting shape, and pipe-cleaner pieces.

2. Peekaboo Kitty Chain & Space Chain

⏱ Time: 25 min each 👶 Age: 4+ ⭐ Difficulty: Easy

🛒 Supplies — Peekaboo Kitty Chain

  • Long strip of purple paper
  • Markers
  • Gems, pearls, pom-poms, and other "doodads"
  • Glue stick
  • Scissors

🛒 Supplies — Space Chain

  • Colorful pipe cleaners
  • Aluminum foil (gold, silver, etc.)
  • Ribbon (red, gold, green, gold-star)
  • Pom-poms

Peekaboo Kitty Chain — Steps

  1. Fold the purple paper accordion-style: under, over, under, over.
  2. On the top fold, draw a kitty silhouette: round body at the bottom, arm reaching up to the very edge of the paper, kitty head, then back down for the other arm to the other edge. The arms must touch the edges so the chain stays connected.
  3. Cut around the silhouette while keeping the paper folded. Do not cut the arm-edges off!
  4. Unfold — you'll have a row of kitties holding paws.
  5. Draw a face on each kitty.
  6. Decorate each one differently: gem necklace, pom-pom bow tie, pearl, sequins, etc.

Space Chain — Steps

  1. Take a pipe cleaner and bend it into a circle — that's a planet.
  2. Crumple a small piece of foil around the loose ends to make it stay closed.
  3. Make more shapes from other pipe cleaners: crescent moons, stars, swirly galaxies.
  4. For a shooting star, attach short ribbons (red, gold, green, star-print) to a star shape so it has a colorful "tail."
  5. Add pom-poms and polka-dot shapes for variety.
  6. To connect everything: take one shape and twist a fresh pipe cleaner around it, then twist the other end to the next shape. Repeat to grow the chain.
💡 Tip: Multiple kids can make their own chains and twist them all together to make one giant party banner.
👩‍🦰 Grown-Up Helper: Cutting the folded kitty silhouette so the connecting arms stay intact.

3. Kitty Pirate Ship

⏱ Time: 45–60 min 👶 Age: 5+ ⭐ Difficulty: Medium

🛒 Supplies

  • 1 cardboard box (shoebox-sized works)
  • 1 paper towel roll (the mast)
  • 1 toilet paper roll (steering wheel base)
  • White canvas paper or cardstock (sail)
  • White craft foam
  • Markers (white + assorted)
  • Ruler
  • Glue (stick + a stronger glue if you have it)
  • Blue pipe cleaner (anchor)
  • Quarter and dime (for tracing portholes)

Steps

  1. Cut off three of the four top flaps of the cardboard box. Save the fourth — it becomes the kitty ears later.
  2. Cut the front of the box down the middle, then trim it so the front comes to a triangle point. Glue the two halves back together at the point — that's the bow.
  3. Trim the top edges so the front sits lower than the back, giving it a classic ship shape.
  4. Draw windows on the sides and have a grown-up cut them out.
  5. Take that 4th flap you saved and cut it into two big triangle "kitty ears." Glue them to the back deck of the ship.
  6. Use a ruler and white marker to draw 4 horizontal lines along the side of the ship.
  7. Trace 5 circles (using a quarter and a dime) on the second line — these are the portholes.
  8. Make the anchor: bend a blue pipe cleaner into an anchor shape, poke a hole near the front of the ship, and slot it through.
  9. Glue the toilet paper roll standing up on the deck — that's the steering wheel base.
  10. Cut a steering-wheel shape from white foam (a circle with spokes) and glue it to the top of the toilet paper roll.
  11. Build the sail: on canvas paper, glue a half-circle (bottom), a square (middle), and a small triangle flag (top). Use a brown marker to draw a line connecting all three.
  12. Cut two thin cardboard strips and glue one across the top and one across the bottom of the sail to keep it stiff.
  13. Glue the sail to the paper towel roll (the mast), then glue the mast standing up in the middle of the ship.
👩‍🦰 Grown-Up Helper: All cardboard cutting (front, top edges, ears, windows, anchor hole). Use safety scissors or a craft knife.

4. Palm Tree Cooling Fan

⏱ Time: 40 min + drying 👶 Age: 5+ ⭐ Difficulty: Medium

🛒 Supplies

  • 3 sheets of green paper (palm fronds)
  • 1 paper towel roll (the trunk)
  • 1 wooden dowel (the fan handle)
  • Brown pom-poms (coconuts)
  • Tape
  • Bendable wire (for the pull mechanism)
  • Yellow air-dry clay (sand island)
  • Light blue craft foam (towel)
  • Glue stick

Steps

  1. Make 3 leaves: take each green sheet and fold it accordion-style end to end.
  2. Wrap a piece of tape around one end of each fold to keep it pinched — this gives each leaf a fan shape.
  3. Pick one leaf to be the "main" leaf. Have a grown-up poke two holes near the pinched end.
  4. Thread a piece of wire through the bottom hole and the wooden dowel through the top hole. (Pulling the wire makes the leaf flap = the fan.)
  5. On the paper towel roll (the trunk), cut 5 slits at the top — these will hold the leaves.
  6. Slot the two non-wire leaves into outer slits and glue them to the back of the roll for stability.
  7. Slot the main leaf (with wire + dowel) into the middle slit. The dowel rests in the slits next to it.
  8. Glue brown pom-poms onto the trunk just below the leaves to make coconuts.
  9. Shape yellow clay into an oval and press a hole in the top where the trunk will sit. Let it fully dry.
  10. Once dry, glue the trunk into the clay hole.
  11. Cut a small rectangle of light blue foam — the beach towel. Glue it to the island next to the tree.
  12. Pull the wire — the main leaf flaps and creates a breeze!
💡 Tip: Air-dry clay needs several hours to a full day to harden. Plan ahead.
👩‍🦰 Grown-Up Helper: Poking the leaf holes, cutting paper-towel slits.

5. Fluffy Hamster Kitties

⏱ Time: 15 min each 👶 Age: 3+ ⭐ Difficulty: Very easy

🛒 Supplies (per hamster kitty)

  • 1 large pom-pom (body) — try beige, gray, white, or pink
  • 4 tiny pom-poms (hands & feet)
  • Craft foam in coordinating colors (ears, nose)
  • Felt scraps (mouth, spots)
  • Googly eyes
  • Tacky glue or strong craft glue (pom-poms don't bond well with glue sticks)

Steps

  1. Glue 2 googly eyes to the front of the big pom-pom.
  2. Glue 2 tiny pom-poms at the bottom (feet) and 2 near the top of the body (hands).
  3. Cut 3 small triangles from craft foam: 2 ears + 1 nose.
  4. Glue the 2 ear-triangles to the top of the head and the nose-triangle just under the eyes.
  5. Cut a small smile shape from felt and glue it under the nose.

Variations from the video

  • Gray hamster kitty: add small gray foam strips/spots to the body.
  • Pink & white hamster kitty: glue a pink felt spot on top of the head, then glue the left ear over the spot so it has a pink ear.
💡 Tip: Make a whole family — gray, white, pink — and build a little box "house" for them.

6. DJ Catnip Popsicle-Stick Puzzle

⏱ Time: 25–35 min 👶 Age: 4+ ⭐ Difficulty: Easy

🛒 Supplies

  • 7 wooden craft sticks (popsicle sticks)
  • Tape (masking or painters)
  • Pencil
  • Markers (red, purple, rainbow set, green)
  • Glue
  • "Doodads": pom-poms, sequins, star/music-note stickers

Steps

  1. Lay 7 sticks side by side, edges touching, to form a square.
  2. Tape across the top, middle, and bottom to hold the sticks together temporarily.
  3. Flip the sticks over (taped side down).
  4. With a pencil, sketch DJ Catnip (or any Gabby Cat) across all 7 sticks. Pencil lets you erase mistakes.
  5. Color his hoodie red.
  6. Color his body purple — but skip the stripes for now.
  7. Color the stripes in rainbow colors.
  8. Color his face and add the green background.
  9. Glue on doodads — pom-poms, sequins, and music-note stickers (DJ Catnip loves music!).
  10. Once dry, peel the tape off the back.
  11. Mix up the sticks and try to put the picture back together!
💡 Tip: Save the tape for next time — re-tape the back to "reset" the puzzle and play again.

7. Caturn — the Kitty Planet (Papier-Mâché)

⏱ Time: 30 min hands-on + overnight dry 👶 Age: 5+ ⭐ Difficulty: Medium (messy!)

🛒 Supplies

  • 1 round balloon
  • Newspaper or scrap paper, torn into strips
  • Flour
  • Water
  • Big mixing bowl + whisk
  • Craft felt (for ears)
  • Glue
  • Markers + paint (blue + pink work great)
  • Pipe cleaners (whiskers)

Steps

  1. Blow up the balloon and tie it off.
  2. In the bowl, mix roughly 1 part flour to 1 part water with the whisk until smooth — no lumps. This is your papier-mâché paste.
  3. Dip each paper strip in the paste, slide it between two fingers to squeeze off the extra goo, and lay it flat onto the balloon.
  4. Cover the entire balloon with overlapping strips. Set aside to dry completely (overnight is safest).
  5. Once dry, cut 2 triangles from felt and glue them to the top — kitty ears.
  6. Paint the whole thing blue (including the ears).
  7. Add pink swirls all over for Caturn's "rings."
  8. Draw a kitty face on the front with marker.
  9. Cut pipe cleaners in half and glue 4 onto the face as whiskers.
💡 Tip: Lay newspaper down everywhere — papier-mâché is gloriously messy.
👩‍🦰 Grown-Up Helper: Mixing the paste, blowing up the balloon, cutting felt and pipe cleaners.

8. Pandy Paws Ear Headband

⏱ Time: 20 min 👶 Age: 4+ ⭐ Difficulty: Easy

🛒 Supplies

  • Plain plastic or fabric headband
  • White ribbon (long enough to wrap the headband)
  • White craft foam (thick) — for the ears
  • Pink sparkly/glitter paper — for the inner ear
  • Black marker
  • Glue

Steps

  1. Wrap the white ribbon all the way around the headband, gluing the ends to hold it.
  2. Cut a thin strip of white foam and glue it along the inside of the headband for padding/comfort.
  3. Cut 2 large triangles from white foam — these are the outer ears.
  4. Cut 2 smaller triangles from pink glitter paper. Glue these on top of the white triangles to make the inner ear.
  5. Glue the finished ears to the top of the headband, evenly spaced.
  6. With a black marker, draw a black stripe across the tip of each ear (Pandy's signature mark).
💡 Tip: Add tiny gems near the base of the ears for extra sparkle.

9. Calming Glitter Globe

⏱ Time: 15 min 👶 Age: 3+ ⭐ Difficulty: Very easy

🛒 Supplies

  • 1 clear jar with a tight-fitting lid (mason or baby-food jar works)
  • Permanent marker
  • Blue pipe cleaners (whiskers)
  • Colored paper (kitty ears)
  • Glue stick
  • Water (to fill the jar)
  • Fine glitter (lots!)

Steps

  1. Flip the jar upside down so the lid is on the bottom (the rounded base becomes the kitty's "face").
  2. With the marker, draw eyes, a nose, and a smile on one side of the jar.
  3. Cut a blue pipe cleaner into 4 short whisker pieces. Glue 2 to each side of the face.
  4. Turn the jar right-side up. Fill it nearly full with water.
  5. Pour in glitter — be generous!
  6. Have a grown-up tighten the lid hard so nothing spills.
  7. Cut 2 triangles from colored paper and glue them onto the lid (which is now the bottom-up "ears" if displayed face-down, or the top of the head if face-up — kid's choice).
  8. Shake gently. Watch the glitter swirl and settle.
💡 Calm-Down Use: Baby Box uses this as a breathing tool — shake, then breathe in slowly while the glitter falls, breathe out as it settles. Great for big feelings.
👩‍🦰 Grown-Up Helper: Tightening the jar lid to prevent leaks. Consider adding a strip of hot glue or duct tape around the rim for extra security.
